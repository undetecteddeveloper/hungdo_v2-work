## Sound effects & music (ASSETS-AUDIO)

SFX in `assets/sound/` are 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz WAVs made with `tools/extract_audio.py curate`
(leading/trailing silence trimmed, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS; `shield_break` is two clips layered with
light saturation). `new_record.wav` is original, generated by `tools/synth_sfx.py`.
Licenses were confirmed on each source page (Kenney asset pages list "Creative Commons CC0";
the OpenGameArt pages list "CC0").
